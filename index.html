After the accident

What happens once your car is in our hands

You should never wonder where your car is in the process. Here is exactly how a repair moves through our shop.

  1. 1

    Estimate

    Bring the car by or have it towed in. We photograph and write up the damage, free, in person, no appointment needed.

  2. 2

    Insurance approval

    We send our estimate to your carrier, meet their appraiser, and negotiate any supplements. You do not chase anyone.

  3. 3

    Teardown & parts

    We disassemble the damaged area to find hidden damage, then order every part before bodywork begins so nothing stalls mid-repair.

  4. 4

    Body & frame

    Panels are repaired or replaced and structural points are pulled back to factory spec on our computerized measuring system.

  5. 5

    Paint & bake

    Computer matched PPG waterborne color, applied in a downdraft booth and baked to a factory grade finish.

  6. 6

    Reassembly & QC

    Trim, lights and sensors go back on, systems are checked, panel gaps are set, and the repair gets a final inspection.

  7. 7

    Detail & delivery

    Every car is cleaned inside and out as a courtesy before you pick it up. You get your car back looking like the accident never happened.

Insurance

You pick the shop. Not your insurer

In Massachusetts, the law is on your side: the choice of repair shop always belongs to you. When an insurance company steers you toward their preferred shop, that is a suggestion, not a rule.

Tell your carrier your car is going to Anchor Auto Body and we handle the rest, from A to Z. We write the estimate, meet the appraiser, negotiate supplements for hidden damage, and arrange your rental through Enterprise or Hertz right here in Woburn.

About Anchor

A Woburn shop since 1971

Anchor Auto Body opened on Breed Avenue in 1971 and has been family run ever since.
