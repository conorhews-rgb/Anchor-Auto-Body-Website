The booth

Prep is 90 percent of a perfect finish

Paint does not hide flaws, it magnifies them. Every panel we refinish is stripped of damage, planed straight, primed, block sanded and sealed before a drop of color goes on. Then it is sprayed in a downdraft booth and baked, the same process the factory used the first time.

  • PPG waterborne basecoat, the same chemistry used by manufacturers
  • Computer assisted color matching refined against your actual car
  • Blended into adjacent panels, never painted to a hard edge
  • Daylight color check on every car before delivery

Questions

Paint questions we hear every week

We pull your factory paint code, then refine it with computer assisted color matching against your actual car, because real paint shifts with age and sun. The final mix is sprayed on a test card and checked against the panel in daylight before it touches your vehicle.

PPG waterborne basecoat uses water instead of solvent as its carrier. It is what most factories spray on new cars, it lays down flatter, the color is more accurate, and it is far cleaner for the air in Woburn. We topcoat it with a catalyzed urethane clear for durability.
