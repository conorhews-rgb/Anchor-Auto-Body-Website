Why it matters

A crooked car is never just cosmetic

Even a few millimeters of structural misalignment shows up everywhere: the car pulls on the highway, tires wear on one shoulder, doors catch, and the crumple zones no longer collapse the way the engineers designed. That is why we measure instead of eyeballing, and why the printout goes in your file.

  • Three dimensional measurement of factory reference points
  • Hydraulic pulls verified against manufacturer data
  • Before and after documentation you can keep
  • Alignment checked before the car is reassembled, not after
