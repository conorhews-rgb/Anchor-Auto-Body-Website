How it works

Watch a dent come out

A PDR tech works the metal from behind in passes, shrinking the dent until the panel reads flat under the light board. Press play to see the idea.

Panel shown with a door ding under shop light

What the tech is doing

Special-shaped rods reach behind the panel through factory openings. Working under a striped light board, the tech pushes the low spots up and taps the high spots down in dozens of tiny passes until the reflection runs straight across the panel again.

  • No filler, no primer, no repaint
  • Factory finish and corrosion protection preserved
  • Usually far faster than conventional repair
  • Ideal for door dings, hail and soft creases
